# Landing Page

This glossary defines the product language for Ludgero Junior's personal landing page. It keeps the page direction precise while separating professional positioning from supporting personal context.

## Language

**Professional identity page**:
A landing page whose main purpose is to establish Ludgero's professional identity, credibility, and contact path.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, sales page, gamer hub

**Public name**:
The name used as the page's primary identity signal: Ludgero Junior.
_Avoid_: Full legal name as headline

**Professional contact**:
A visitor evaluating Ludgero in a work context, such as a recruiter, client, employer, colleague, or IT/public-sector contact.
_Avoid_: Follower, viewer, player

**Human-side section**:
A secondary section that shows Ludgero's interests, community involvement, and personality without competing with the professional message.
_Avoid_: Personal homepage, gamer homepage

**Human hierarchy**:
The personal-section order of technology, development, games, communities, and Guild ARKHAM, presented after professional contact.
_Avoid_: Second main page, gamer landing page

**Primary CTA**:
The single most important action the page should guide visitors toward.
_Avoid_: Button cluster, social links

**Professional proof points**:
The five concise credibility themes used to explain Ludgero's work style: problem solving, specialized technical support, reliability, user support, and continuous learning.
_Avoid_: Skill list, resume bullets

**Professional progression story**:
A concise narrative of Ludgero's evolution at TRE-SC, from N1 support through N2 responsibilities into specialized support, problem solving, documentation, and technical assistance.
_Avoid_: Full resume timeline, technology inventory

**N1**:
The initial support stage in Ludgero's TRE-SC trajectory, described for visitors as atendimento e suporte inicial.
_Avoid_: Help desk only

**N2**:
The second-level support stage in Ludgero's TRE-SC trajectory, described for visitors as suporte tecnico especializado.
_Avoid_: Generic support

**Professional hero**:
The first viewport of the page, focused on Ludgero Junior, the Analista de Sistemas Senior title, a strong 15-years-at-TRE-SC credibility signal, and the primary email CTA.
_Avoid_: Intro card, cover section

**Page structure**:
The final landing page order: Hero, Experiencia no TRE-SC, Como contribuo no dia a dia, Contato, and Alem do trabalho.
_Avoid_: Two equal homepages, resume timeline

**Own visual identity**:
The page's restrained visual language, using light backgrounds, discreet blue and green accents, strong contrast, and high legibility without copying TRE-SC's official identity.
_Avoid_: TRE-SC visual identity, generic corporate theme

**Personal links**:
Links related to Ludgero's interests and communities, such as Instagram, Twitch, and Guild ARKHAM, visually separated from professional contact actions.
_Avoid_: Professional CTAs, main contact options

**Professional contact hierarchy**:
The contact order where email is the primary action and LinkedIn is the secondary professional action.
_Avoid_: Equal-weight social links

**Analista de Sistemas Senior**:
The primary professional title for Ludgero's page, reflecting a systems role broader than technical support alone.
_Avoid_: Analista de Suporte Senior, Senior Systems Analyst

**TRE-SC professional context**:
The long-term professional setting where Ludgero has worked for 15 years and where his experience should be primarily anchored.
_Avoid_: Employer, client logo

**Contractual employer**:
The service provider formally employing Ludgero at a given time, currently Wyntech, while his long-term role remains tied to TRE-SC.
_Avoid_: Main professional context

**Public-sector boundary**:
The page's constraint that TRE-SC experience may be described, but no wording or visual identity should imply official endorsement or institutional representation.
_Avoid_: Official page, endorsed profile
